## Highlights

Follow-up on tag **`firmware-v1.2.18`** / `#FW-64`. That release already appends waterfall and offload counters to the debug-log heartbeat (`rec seg lost evic wrows full ofok offail`). This release **does not replace those keys**. It appends three complementary fields and gives `dbglog_hb` enough stack to format the longer line.

**What 1.2.18 already has**

- Flash-side progress: `wrows` (`flash_rows`), `full` (`flash_full`)
- Completed-segment counters: `seg`, `lost`, `evic`
- Offload totals: `ofok`, `offail`

**What this adds (appended; `#FW-64` names and order unchanged)**

| Key | Source | Why it is not redundant |
|---|---|---|
| `ring=count/capacity` | `spectrogram_get_status()` | RAM spectrogram ring fill. Distinct from LittleFS `wrows`/`full`. |
| `st=` | `wf_offload_get_stat().last_status` | Last HTTP status, or a negative transport error. Distinguishes a latched `offail` counter from a receiver that is healthy again. |
| `stk=` | `uxTaskGetStackHighWaterMark(NULL)` | Remaining stack of `dbglog_hb`, **bytes** on ESP32-S3 (`StackType_t` is `uint8_t`). Not present in `/api/*`. |

`stk` is sampled **before** `snprintf` / `write_raw`. The first heartbeat after boot is therefore the pre-format watermark; later ticks include that frame. Do not treat the first sample as the steady-state minimum.

**Stack**

`dbglog_hb` is raised **3072 → 4096**. After `#FW-64` lengthened the format string, a 3072-byte task left about **740** bytes after the first `snprintf` (below a 768-byte lab gate). With 4096, post-format HWM started at **1764** and settled at **1456** under load.

## 72-hour soak — PASSED

A **72-hour representative soak passed** on ESP32-S3 N16R8 with these diagnostics and the 4096-byte stack:

- Waterfall recording stayed on; USB analyzer and Wi-Fi STA stayed connected; HTTP offload kept sending.
- No unexpected reboot; uptime was monotonic.
- `stk` stayed at **1456** bytes on heartbeats after the first (comfort band ≥1024; fail gate was 768).
- `lost` / `seg_lost` did not increase during the window.
- Debug-log ring `drop` stayed 0; heap minimum watermark did not slide.
- Heartbeats continued on the 60 s cadence (no gaps >90 s in the pulled ring).

This soak validates the stack bump and the extra fields under load. It is **not** a claim that stock 1.2.18 (3072, no `stk`) was observed for 72 hours.
